Field notebook: vocabulary
  • Paleolithic: Old Stone Age; people relied on hunting, gathering, and fishing rather than farming.
  • Neolithic: New Stone Age, associated in many regions with farming and domesticated plants and animals.
  • Hunter-gatherer: someone who obtains food from wild plants and animals.
  • Nomadic: moving from place to place rather than living permanently in one location.
  • Domestication: a long process in which plants or animals change through their relationship with people.
  • Surplus: more than is immediately needed.
  • Specialization: focusing on a particular kind of work.
  • Metallurgy: working with metals.

Different ways of life could overlap. Neither lifestyle makes people less intelligent or less cultural.
